What can FINOVA do for you?

Intelligent Profile & Goal Parsing

Instantly structure your financial profile—income, expenses, assets, liabilities, time horizon, and risk tolerance—from natural language while automatically highlighting missing details.

Multi-Scenario Cash Flow Modeling

Compare three distinct financial trajectories (Baseline, Conservative, and Optimistic) powered by precise mathematical calculation models rather than unpredictable AI guesses.

Real-Time "What-If" & Stress Testing

Interactively adjust savings rates, target deadlines, or simulate economic shocks (such as a sudden income drop or expense surge) to evaluate your financial resilience in real time.

AI Trade-Off & Risk Insights

Receive clear, objective AI explanations detailing potential trade-offs, risk limits, and opportunity costs across scenarios—helping you make confident, well-informed decisions.

How to use FINOVA

Create an account

  1. Move your mouse over the person icon in the top-right corner (on a phone, tap it).
  2. Choose Register.
  3. Enter your full name, email, a password (at least 8 characters, with letters and numbers) and the same password again.
  4. Click Register. You are signed in automatically and a green dot appears on the person icon.

Log in

  1. Hover over (or tap) the person icon.
  2. Choose Log in.
  3. Type the email and password you registered with. Use Show to check what you typed.
  4. Click Log in. You return to the home page and your name appears in the account menu.

Forgot your password

  1. On the Log in page, click Forgot password?
  2. Enter your registered email and click Verify email.
  3. Type a new password twice and click Save new password.
  4. Log in with the new password.

Log out

  1. Hover over (or tap) the person icon. Your name is shown at the top of the menu.
  2. Click Log out. Your session ends immediately.

On a shared computer, always log out when you finish.

Light and dark mode

  1. Click the moon icon to switch to dark mode. It turns into a sun.
  2. Click the sun icon to return to light mode.
